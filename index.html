Meet the Chef

Rooted in intention,
driven by flavor

I'm Talia — a private chef based in New York City with a passion for food that does more than fill a plate. Every menu I create starts with whole, seasonal ingredients and ends with something that feels like fine dining but nourishes like home.

With years of experience across private kitchens, wellness-focused dining, and elevated entertaining, I bring a unique perspective: food should be beautiful, healthful, and deeply satisfying — all at once.
